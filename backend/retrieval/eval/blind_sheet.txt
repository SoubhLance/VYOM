[01] (conceptual) What does the Gita say about acting without attachment to results?
Pool size: 12
  [x]   3.25: Just as the ignorant, attached to their work, act, O Arjuna, so too the learned should act without any attachment, and only for the welfare of the world.
  [~]   3.18: No purpose is served for him by what he has done or by what he has not done. For him, there is hardly any dependence on any purpose among all beings.
  [x]   18.9: When actions are performed as they ought to be done, O Arjuna, renouncing attachment and the results thereof, such abandonment is regarded as Sattvika.
  [x]    3.9: In this world, people are fettered by their actions, unless they are performed as a sacrifice. Therefore, O Arjuna, let your actions be done without attachment, as a sacrifice only.
  [~]   4.14: Actions do not stain me, nor do I have a desire for the fruits of them either. Whoever understands me in this way is not bound by their actions.
  [x]   3.19: Therefore, do your work without attachment, which ought to be done. For, a person who works without attachment attains the Supreme.
  [x]   5.10: He who acts without attachment, reposing all actions on Brahman (Prakṛti), is untouched by evil, just as a lotus leaf is untouched by water.
  [x]   2.47: Your right is only to work, but not to its results; do not let the results of action be your motive, nor let your attachment be to inaction.
  [x]   2.48: Abandoning attachment and established in Yoga, perform works, viewing success and failure with an even mind. Evenness of mind is said to be Yoga.
  [x]  18.23: The daily obligatory action which is performed without attachment and without likes or dislikes, by one who does not hanker for rewards, is said to be born of sattva.
  [x]   18.6: It is My decided and final view that even these acts should be done, O Arjuna, with relinquishment of attachment and the forsaking of the fruits thereof.
  [x]   4.20: Having given up attachment to the results of action, he who is ever-content, dependent on nothing, does not really do anything even though he is engaged in action.
